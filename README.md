[Lui Ledger](https://luiledger.com) is a personal bookkeeping desktop application that keeps your bookkeeping on your own machine, encrypted, with dashboards you customize.
No cloud account, no subscription.

It is for people who want real, accurate bookkeeping without
handing their financial data to a cloud service. You customize the dashboards and widgets you come
back to: their layout, details, and settings. It is built on [double-entry
bookkeeping](https://luiledger.com/docs/double-entry-bookkeeping), the same accounting method professionals use to
keep assets, liabilities, equity, income, and spending in balance.

## Why Lui Ledger exists

Most budgeting apps either oversimplify (a single list of money in and out) or require a
subscription and an internet connection to work at all. Lui Ledger takes a different approach: proper
double-entry accounting, running entirely on your own machine, with dashboards you customize. Drill
into one expense category or account, zoom out for the bigger picture, and move across a month, a
year, or a specific range

## Principles

### Offline by default

Day-to-day bookkeeping should not depend on someone else's server. Your ledger lives on your device.
Lui Ledger works without an internet connection.

### Encrypted, not just "private"

Privacy is not a login screen on a remote database. Data is encrypted at rest and protected by your
password. Lock the app and the files themselves are unreadable.

### The view is yours

How you look at your books is yours to decide. Lui Ledger does not lock you into one home screen we
designed. You shape the dashboards and widgets you come back to.

### Change scale, keep context

Books have two axes: the account tree and time. You should be able to change scale on both without
starting over. Drill into one category or account, zoom out for the bigger picture, or move across a
month, a year, or a range.

### Accounting-first thinking

Lui Ledger is built on double-entry bookkeeping: every transaction must balance, so money cannot
appear or disappear. Calculations follow the same account principles as [hledger](https://hledger.org/)
and [ledger-cli](https://ledger-cli.org/), the rules professional bookkeepers use: accounts, balanced
postings, commodities, and prices. Lui Ledger will not save an edit if a balance check or assertion
fails.

Lui Ledger presents that through a modern interface rather than a command line.
